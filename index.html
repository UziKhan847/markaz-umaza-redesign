Welcome to Markaz Umaza

A home for learning the Book of Allah and its language

We teach Arabic, Qur'an recitation with Tajwid, Hifdh and basic Islamic studies through structured online classes, for a growing community of students in Canada, the US and around the world.

Our students have gone on to win local Qur'an competitions, a reflection of steady, caring teaching.

Dedicated instructors

Teachers of Arabic, Tajwid, Hifdh and Islamic knowledge who follow each student closely.

Structured lessons

Clear levels and a planned curriculum, so every class builds on the last.

Learn anytime

24/7 access to course materials, so you can review between live classes.

Certificate

Finish your course and receive a certificate of completion.
